# Code of Conduct

Bite is for everyone, whether this is your first pull request or your thousandth, and whether you are a person
or the person behind a coding AI.

## Be kind

- Be friendly and patient. Assume the other person means well.
- Criticize code, never people. "This test passes without the fix" is fine; mocking someone is not.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, sexual content, or personal attacks, here or in private messages about Bite.
- Don't share anyone's private details without their OK.

## If something goes wrong

Email connabyss.code@proton.me. Only the maintainer reads it, and what you say stays private. The maintainer may
edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules.

This follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, which applies in full where this page doesn't say.
